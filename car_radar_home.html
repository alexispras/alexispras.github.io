Car Radar

Application Purpose

Car Radar is a mobile application designed specifically for drivers. Its primary purpose is to automatically save your car's parking position and help you find your vehicle easily using an interactive map and a built-in radar interface.

Why We Use Google OAuth (Google API Services)

Car Radar requests access to your Google Account (OAuth) strictly for basic authentication. We only request access to your basic profile information (email address) to securely identify you as a user and to link your app settings and premium features to your account.

Compliance & Terms of Service

The Car Radar application strictly complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service. The application does not use Google APIs to generate or manipulate images, and it strictly prohibits and does not facilitate any AI-generated Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII).
