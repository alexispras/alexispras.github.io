Car Radar

App Purpose:

Car Radar is a comprehensive driving assistant application that automatically saves your car's parking position. It helps users easily navigate back to their parked vehicle using GPS tracking, interactive maps, and a built-in radar interface.

Google Account Integration:

Car Radar requests access to Google Authentication (OAuth) to allow users to securely log in and sync their parking history. This ensures that users do not lose their saved parking locations if they change their device or reinstall the application.
