This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use my Service.

Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, I may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. Service uses the following information for its operation:

  • Foreground location of car parking lot using GPS
  • Background location of car parking lot using GPS
  • Activity Recognition data as provided by Android OS
  • Start time and duration of car parking

Google API Services and User Data

Our Service allows users to authenticate using Google Sign-In (OAuth). If you choose to log in with your Google Account, the App will request access to basic profile information (such as your email address) solely for the purpose of authenticating your account and securely syncing your parking data across your devices.

Limited Use Policy: Car Radar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use your Google data for unauthorized purposes, and we do not sell or share your Google Account data with third parties.

Foreground & Background Location Data

This application uses Google location services to obtain device location when the application is in the foreground. This information is used to save the parking location of your car only and remains on your device.

Application collects location data in the background even when the app is closed or not in use. It uses Google location services for this. By accepting this permission you allow app to collect background location data and process it. This data is used only to determine GPS location of your parked car and nothing else. When app receives message from Activity Recognition that you leave your car, Service sends GPS location request in the background to get location of your car to save. This is the only case when the app requests background location. It remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

The application does not store your location data on our servers. All location data collected by the Service is stored entirely locally on your device. You have full control over your data. You can delete all your collected data at any time by simply uninstalling the "Car Radar" app from your device or by clearing the app's data via your device's system settings.
